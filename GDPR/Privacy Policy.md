## Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: 08-01-2025**

At Brewing Stand, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we store and protect it. By using Brewing Stand, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

### 1\. Data We Collect

We collect the following types of data:

- **GitHub Authentication Data**:
  - **GitHub ID**: We use your GitHub ID to authenticate you and enable you to log in to Brewing Stand.
  - **Username**: We use your GitHub username to set up your username within the Brewing Stand application.
  - **Avatar**: We use your GitHub avatar to provide an image for your user profile.
- **Project Data**:
  - **User-Generated Content**: When you create, update, or delete Minecraft recipes within Brewing Stand, this data (e.g., project name, description, and content) is stored.
  - **Metadata**: Information related to project creation, updates, and user interactions with projects is stored.

### 2\. Why We Collect Your Data

We collect your data for the following purposes:

- **Authentication**: To authenticate users via GitHub and provide a personalized user experience within Brewing Stand.
- **Project Management**: To allow users to create, update, and delete projects that contain Minecraft recipes.
- **Service Improvement**: To improve the user experience and functionality of Brewing Stand.

### 3\. How Long We Retain Your Data

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to provide the services of Brewing Stand, including:

- **GitHub Authentication Data**: Retained for the duration of your account use, unless you decide to disconnect your GitHub account.
- **Project Data**: Retained as long as the project exists in your account. You can delete your projects at any time, and this will remove the associated project data from our system.
- **Data Storage Duration**: If you delete your account or projects, we will retain the data for a reasonable period in case of accidental deletion or for legal obligations, but it will not be used actively.  

Note: The deletion of an entire user account has not yet been implemented. However, plans for implementing this feature are in place.

### 4\. Who Has Access to Your Data

- **Brewing Stand Team**: Authorized personnel within Brewing Stand may access your data only when necessary to provide support or to ensure the service functions properly.
- **Third-Party Service Providers**: We use third-party cloud services, such as Azure and Cosmos, to store your project data. These services have access to your data only to the extent necessary to provide their services.

### 5\. How Your Data Is Stored and Secured

- **Storage**: Your personal data and project data are stored in a secure cloud database (PostgreSQL) and Azure Blob Storage, both located in West Germany.
- **Security**: We employ industry-standard security practices, such as encryption in transit, to protect your data. Project content will be encrypted in the future.

Note: The encryption of the Project content is still planned and not yet implemented as the current implementation does not use project contents.

- **Access Control**: We restrict access to your data to authorized personnel and third-party service providers only when necessary.

### 6\. Your Rights Regarding Your Data

As a user of Brewing Stand, you have the following rights:

- **Access**: You can request to view the data we have collected about you.
- **Rectification**: You can update your personal information, such as your username and avatar.
- **Deletion**: You can delete your account and all associated project data at any time.
- **Portability**: You can request to receive your personal data in a machine-readable format.
- **Withdrawal of Consent**: If you consented to the processing of your data, you can withdraw your consent at any time by disconnecting your GitHub account from Brewing Stand.

### 7\. Data Transfer

Your data is stored in data centers located in West Germany. If data is transferred to other locations, we will ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

### 8\. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the updated policy on this page with an updated revision date.
